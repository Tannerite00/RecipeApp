# RecipeHub


## Features
Welcome to RecipeHub! An app with over 1600 vegan whole-food-plant-based recipes. 

On the main page, you can browse and discover recipes from our collection, 
either by searching for the recipe name, or, if you are trying to make do with 
what you have, search for recipes based off ingredients! Just use a comma to separate them
and it will auto-filter recipes that share those ingredients. 

Speaking of filters, hit the filter button to fitler out recipes based on meal type, 
allergens, or sort the recipes alphabetically, by prep-time or cook-time or based on
rating! 

If you already know what you love, you can also filter by which recipes you have favorited!

The recipes can be favorited either from the recipe-details page or on the recipe search page. 
Once you click into a recipe-detail page, you can favorite it, rate it out of 5 stars, leave a
comment, adjust the serving size, and add it to your meal plan. 

On the meal plan page, you can see meal plans for the last few weeks, the current week, and plan 
ahead up to two weeks from this week. You can also adjust the serving size here, add a recipe to a 
day directly with the "Add Recipe" buttons, and change which day a recipe is for by dragging it to a 
new day! 

Meals from the Meal Plan page are directly transposed to the Grocery page. They take into account the 
serving size of each meal, and adjust the groceries accordingly. The grocery page also allows you to 
toggle the organization to be by recipe or group them by ingredient type. When clicking the ingredients, 
they will cross themselves off and there is a progress bar at the top that will celebrate you completing it! 
This also helps you in not forgetting an item on the list, since you can see your progression at the top. 

Remember, if there is no confetti, you missed something! 

Each user has an account, and in it you can find your favorites, recipes you have rated, and recipes you have commented on. 


## Offline support

The app caches recipes and ratings in your browser the first time they
load. If you reopen the app without internet, the cached data is used so
browsing still works. Any ratings or comments you leave while offline are queued
locally and synced automatically the next time you have connectivity. 
If you create mealplans offline, they will show while offline and update when back online.
